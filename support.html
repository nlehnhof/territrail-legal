Support

TerriTRAIL · operated by Nathan Lehnhof

Get help or report a bug

In the app, open Profile → Send Feedback / Report a Bug. Include what you were doing and your device model. This is the fastest way to reach us.

No account or can’t sign in? Open a request on our public tracker: github.com/nlehnhof/territrail-legal/issues. Please do not post personal information there.

Report a player or username

On the leaderboard, tap the “…” next to a player and choose Report this player. You can also choose Hide this player. We review reports and remove names or accounts that break the Terms of Use.

Delete your account and data

Open Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, runs and history right away. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Location problems

  • Allow location (“While Using” or “Always”) for TerriTRAIL in iOS Settings → Privacy → Location Services.
  • Keep Precise Location on, or runs won’t claim tiles accurately.
  • Movement faster than running speed is rejected as a cheating safeguard.
See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
